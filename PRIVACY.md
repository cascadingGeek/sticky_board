# Sticky Board Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 17 November 2026_

Sticky Board is a Chrome extension that shows your sticky notes on the new tab page and lets you pin notes on websites. It is built so that your notes never leave your browser.

## What Sticky Board stores

- **Your notes**: titles, text, checklists, colors, positions, due times and reminder settings.
- **Pages you pin notes to**: the address and title of each page where you pin a note, so the note can appear there again.
- **Text and links you capture**: when you use "Send selection to Sticky Board" or "Save link to Sticky Board", the selected text or link (and the page it came from) is saved as a note.
- **Your settings**: theme, handwriting font, sound, timezone and start of week.

## Where it is stored

- Notes are stored in `chrome.storage.local`, on your device only.
- Settings are stored in `chrome.storage.sync`. If you have turned on Chrome sync, Chrome syncs these settings across your own signed-in browsers through your Google account, under Google's privacy policy. Your notes are not synced.

## What Sticky Board does not do

- It does not send any data to the developer or to any server. The extension makes no network requests.
- It has no account, analytics, tracking, advertising or third party code.
- It does not read, record or collect your browsing history. It only reads a page when you choose to pin or view notes on it, and only on sites you have allowed.
- It does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone.

## Site access

Sticky Board asks for permission one site at a time, only when you pin a note on that site. You can remove access at any time from the extension's settings or from Chrome's extension settings.

## Your control

- Edit or delete any note at any time. Deleted notes go to Trash and are removed permanently after a while, or right away if you empty Trash.
- Export a backup file of your notes from Settings. The file is saved to your computer and goes nowhere else.
- Uninstalling the extension removes all of its stored data from your browser.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
